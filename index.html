Each row shows the last 120 four-hour candles, about 4 weeks of price, with the 20, 50 and 200 EMA. A cross of the 20 and 50 is the trend signal. The 200 only shows when price is close to it. The three tags show the 4H 20/50 EMA trend, COT positioning and the macro score. The tags are scored one point each, up or down, with neutral and n/a scoring zero. A net score of 3 is “Strong”, 2 is Long or Short, and 1 is “Weak”. If the points cancel out or point against the trend, it shows Mixed. COT is long or short by its net position, with a neutral zone near zero. A COT tag also says “growing” or “shrinking” when the weekly change is at least 1% of open interest. “Crowded” marks positions in the top or bottom 10% of the last 3 years. Tap a row for the numbers.
